# Building Permit Requirements - New Residential Construction

*Converted from Township of Muskoka Lakes Building Department Guide, Rev. 2024*

## Required Documents

### Site Plan
- Drawn to scale (1:500 or 1:200)
- Show all existing and proposed structures
- Dimensions from all structures to lot lines and high water mark
- Septic system location (if applicable)
- Driveway and parking area
- Well location (if applicable)

### Architectural Drawings
- Floor plans (all levels), drawn to scale
- All four elevations
- Building cross-section
- Foundation plan
- Roof plan with drainage

### Engineering
- Stamped structural drawings required for:
  - Spans over 4.8 m
  - Cantilevered elements
  - Roof loads exceeding standard residential
- Geotechnical report required if within 30 m of a steep slope or if soil conditions are uncertain

### Septic
- Septic permit must be obtained separately from the Health Unit before building permit can be issued
- Minimum 30 m from any water body
- Minimum 15 m from any well

## Fees

| Project Value | Fee |
|---|---|
| Up to $50,000 | $500 |
| $50,001 - $200,000 | $500 + 0.8% of value over $50,000 |
| Over $200,000 | $1,700 + 0.6% of value over $200,000 |

## Processing Time

- Standard: 10 business days from complete application
- Complex (requiring variance or referral): 30-60 business days

## Common Reasons for Incomplete Applications

- Missing septic approval
- Site plan not drawn to scale
- Setback dimensions not shown
- No engineer stamp on structural drawings
