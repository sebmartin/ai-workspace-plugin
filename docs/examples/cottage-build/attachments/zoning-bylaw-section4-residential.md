# Zoning Bylaw - Section 4: Shoreline Residential (SR) Zone

*Converted from Township of Muskoka Lakes Zoning Bylaw 2019-50, Section 4*

## 4.1 Permitted Uses

- Single detached dwelling
- Boathouse (one per lot, subject to Section 4.6)
- Accessory buildings and structures
- Home occupation (subject to Section 8.3)

## 4.2 Lot Requirements

| Requirement | Standard |
|---|---|
| Minimum lot area | 0.4 ha |
| Minimum lot frontage (road) | 30 m |
| Minimum water frontage | 45 m |

## 4.3 Yard Setbacks

| Setback | Minimum Distance |
|---|---|
| From high water mark | 30 m |
| From front lot line (road) | 7.5 m |
| From interior side lot line | 3 m |
| From exterior side lot line (corner lot) | 6 m |
| Between principal dwelling and accessory building | 3 m |

## 4.4 Building Height and Coverage

| Requirement | Maximum |
|---|---|
| Building height (principal dwelling) | 9 m |
| Building height (accessory building) | 5 m |
| Total lot coverage (all structures) | 10% |
| Impervious surface coverage | 15% |

## 4.5 Special Provisions

- No structure shall be erected within 30 m of the high water mark of any lake or river
- Shoreline buffers must be maintained as natural vegetation within 15 m of the high water mark
- Exterior lighting must be downward-facing and shielded to minimize light pollution on the water

## 4.6 Boathouse Regulations

- Maximum one boathouse per lot
- Maximum footprint: 75 m²
- Must be located waterward of the high water mark
- No residential use permitted in boathouse
- Maximum height: 4.5 m above dock level
