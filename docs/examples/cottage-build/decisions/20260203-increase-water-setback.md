# Decision: Cottage placement and water setback

**Date**: 2026-02-03
**Thread**: [cottage-build](../README.md)
**Status**: Accepted

## Decision

Place the cottage at the following location:

- **Footprint**: 11 m x 14 m (154 m²)
- **Height**: 7.5 m
- **Water setback**: 34 m from high water mark
- **Road setback**: 9 m from front lot line
- **Side setback**: 4.5 m from east lot line

Water setback is set at 34 m, not the minimum 30 m.

## Relevant Bylaws (Bylaw 2019-50, Section 4.3)

> **From high water mark**: 30 m minimum
> **From front lot line (road)**: 7.5 m minimum
> **From interior side lot line**: 3 m minimum

All setbacks are satisfied. Water setback has 4 m of margin; others have standard margin.

## Why 34 m

Initial placement was 33 m -- compliant, but close. Shifting 1 m further costs nothing in the design and provides margin if the survey comes back slightly different than expected. Reduces the chance of a bylaw officer flagging it during inspection.

## Lot Coverage Check (s.4.4)

> **Total lot coverage (all structures)**: 10% maximum
> **Impervious surface coverage**: 15% maximum

Cottage (154 m²) + driveway (~80 m²) + deck (~40 m²) = 274 m² impervious.
Lot area: 6,000 m². Coverage: 2.6%, impervious: 4.6%. Both well within limits.

## Consequences

- Slightly less lake view from the main floor (negligible at 1 m difference)
- Comfortable buffer for permit review and any future additions
