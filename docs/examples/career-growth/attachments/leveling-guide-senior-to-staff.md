# Leveling Guide: Senior Engineer to Staff Engineer

## Senior Engineer (Current)

- Owns delivery of well-scoped projects within a team
- Identifies technical risks and mitigates proactively
- Reviews code and mentors peers within the team
- Communicates technical decisions clearly to the team

## Staff Engineer (Target)

### Technical Leadership
- Drives architectural decisions that affect multiple teams or systems
- Identifies and resolves ambiguity at the system level, not just the component level
- Produces artifacts (RFCs, design docs, postmortems) that inform decisions org-wide

### Scope and Impact
- Owns problems that span team boundaries
- Initiates projects, not just executes them
- Measurable impact on engineering productivity or system reliability at the platform level

### Influence
- Shapes technical direction through writing and discussion, not just code
- Recognized as a domain expert by peers outside the immediate team
- Unblocks other teams by providing clarity, tooling, or shared infrastructure

### Collaboration
- Works effectively with product, infra, and other engineering teams
- Elevates others: mentorship, code review, design feedback
